Begin with your agreement

Evaluation programs use different profit targets, daily loss calculations, drawdown limits and reset times. Read the terms for your specific account before using any tool to help track a requirement.

Keep the process close

FYNX calculators and prop-mode tools help organize a routine: define your risk, check the session and review the process. Tools support the routine; the accepted program agreement determines the rules.

Review limits carefully

Daily loss and overall drawdown can be measured differently. Floating profit and loss, fees and reset times may matter. Confirm the applicable definition and do not assume a calculator automatically enforces your provider’s requirements.

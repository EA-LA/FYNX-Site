Read the context

Market headlines, chart views and trading sessions serve different purposes. News can explain what is attracting attention; a chart shows price history; session information helps place that activity in time.

Build a repeatable check

Review the market story, look at your chosen chart timeframe and check the relevant session before making a decision. FYNX brings those views close together so the routine is easier to repeat.

Check the source and time

Market information can be delayed or unavailable. Always check the source and timestamp of a quote or story. A chart pattern or headline is context for your research, not a promise of a particular outcome.
